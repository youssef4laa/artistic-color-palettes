# Harbourside Physio - product design principles

Agreed at the team offsite, March. These resolve arguments; if two of them clash, the lower number
wins.

1. **Calm over clever.** Patients booking physio are often in pain or anxious. No saturated colour
   behind running text or labels. Saturated colour is for lines, icons, focus rings and small marks;
   buttons are tinted fills with dark ink. If a design needs to shout, it is the wrong design.
2. **One thing per screen.** Each screen has one job and one obvious next step.
3. **Say it plainly.** Words a patient would use; no clinical jargon, no marketing voice.
4. **Never rely on colour alone.** Every state also has an icon or a word.
5. **Older hands, older eyes.** Targets at least 44px, text at least 16px, contrast comfortably above the minimum.

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners and managers of small mixed farms (2-40 hectares) who keep their records in notebooks and spreadsheets. They use it at a desk in the farm office and on a phone outdoors in bright light.

## Product Purpose
Fieldbook keeps fields, spray records, harvest tallies and compliance exports in one place, so an inspection takes minutes.

## Positioning
Spray-record exports formatted for UK farm assurance schemes, which general spreadsheet tools do not provide.

## Operating Context
Seasonal peaks (spraying, harvest) mean short sessions with dirty hands. Inspections happen with an inspector reading over the user's shoulder.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Web app, responsive. Settings page manages profile, notification preferences and account deletion. Export is PDF and CSV.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Fieldbook. Calm, practical, a field notebook rather than a SaaS dashboard. Palette decided and recorded in DESIGN.md: do not change colours.

## Evidence on Hand
Real field and spray data from two pilot farms (anonymised) in data/pilot/. No testimonials yet; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Records first, decoration never.
- Works with one hand outdoors.
- Plain words a farmer would use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA; bright-sunlight legibility on phones; no information by colour alone.

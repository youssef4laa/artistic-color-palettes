# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Home bakers and cafe owners in the UK who want better flour for sourdough and pastry. They arrive from a newsletter or an Instagram link on a phone, usually in the evening, deciding whether to try a first box.

## Product Purpose
Kettle & Crumb sells a monthly box of small-mill flours with recipe cards. The landing page must get a visitor to start a first box (GBP 24).

## Positioning
Flours come from three named mills and each box includes the miller's notes, which a supermarket subscription cannot truthfully offer.

## Operating Context
Visitors compare against supermarket flour and two competitor boxes. Fulfilment happens on the first Monday of the month.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Single landing page with one primary action (Start a box). Checkout is handled by a hosted payment page. No account system yet.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Kettle & Crumb. Voice: warm, plainspoken, a little proud of the mills. No colour commitments yet; the owner will choose colours from a book of colour combinations.

## Evidence on Hand
Three miller photographs in assets/mills/, a recipe card PDF, two customer quotes with permission. No press yet; do not invent press logos or review counts.

## Product Principles
- Show the mills before the price.
- One action per screen.
- Plain words over bakery cliches.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA for text and controls. Many visitors are over 55 and read on small phone screens.

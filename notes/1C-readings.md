## Uncertain readings and why

1. 色度 (p. 20 / PDF 22) — prewar colour term listed between hue and lightness; read as
   chroma/saturation. Marked `[uncertain]` in wada-method.md §3.4.
2. Different-tone sub-types (p. 20 / PDF 22) — Wada explains the five same-tone sub-types
   individually but gives the different-tone column only a one-sentence gloss; our row readings
   apply the same modifiers by analogy. Marked in §3.5.
3. Triangle aid card placement (p. 23 / PDF 25) — text says the triangle's tip gives the collar
   colour, the adjoining middle chip the robe, the next the sash or coat; how a triangle window
   yields "most area" for the middle chip is not explicit. Card label read at 4.5× as
   切リ取ツテ穴ヲ開ケル (cut out to open a window), so the cards are windows. Marked in §5.4.
4. Furigana over 人為的 (p. 19 / PDF 21) — tiny katakana gloss, looks like コンヴェンショナル
   ("conventional"); not used in the output, so left unmarked there.
5. Resolved: the 2 × 5 table entries (re-rendered at 4.5×, clear); aid-card labels (one) to (three)
   and their shapes (triangle, rectangle, wave); 4-colour plate layout (checked on PDF 152).

## Notes for other agents

- Wada says large areas look paler/weaker than the chips (p. 23–24 / PDF 25–26); this differs from
  the commonly cited modern area effect. wada-method.md reports Wada faithfully and adds a note.
- Plate number ranges: 1–120 pairs, 121–240 triads, 241–348 quads (confirmed by plate 241 on
  PDF 152 and the 120/120/108 split on p. 12 / PDF 14).
- The essay text in the reprint is an excerpt (latter half) of the original booklet, so the "first
  half" on general colour theory is not available in this scan.
